# Production brief

Every style in this catalog is one short film that shows what the style can do. This brief is
the contract each clip is made against: what it must look like, what the files must contain,
and how it is checked before it is merged.

## The clip

- **One film per style**, 8–10 seconds, 1920 × 1080. There is no shared story; pick a subject
  that shows the style off.
- **Recognisable within three seconds.** The signature texture, colour, typography and
  motion must be visible from the first beats.
- **A real piece of animation.** One focus at a time, deliberate composition, motion with
  anticipation, overshoot and stagger where the style calls for it, and at least one scene
  change or transformation.
- **A beginning and an ending.** Open on purpose and close with a fade, a settle or a final
  pose, never a hard cut on the last frame.
- **Everything shown is invented.** Characters, products, brands, publications and places
  are fictional, with generic names. No photos of real people, no stock images, no real
  logos. A style may be inspired by a creator or a tradition; it must not copy their
  characters or artwork.
- **People must read clearly.** If a figure appears, it has a readable head and profile,
  neck and shoulders, a torso, jointed limbs and hands, even as a silhouette. If a figure
  cannot be drawn well, tell the story with objects or animals.
- **English text only**, set in fonts that cover every character on screen.

## The files (`styles/<slug>/`)

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `anim.html` | Draws the film on a 1920 × 1080 `<canvas id="c">`. Exposes `window.ready` (a promise that settles once fonts and precomputed textures are ready) and `window.draw({ t })`, which paints the frame for time `t` in seconds and returns it as base64 JPEG data (`toDataURL('image/jpeg', 0.92)`). Frames depend only on `t`: seeded random numbers only, no clock. |
| `render.mjs`, `events.mjs` | Identical copies of [`tools/render.mjs`](tools/render.mjs) and [`tools/events.mjs`](tools/events.mjs). Never edit the copies; `check_catalog.py` rejects a copy that differs. |
| `render.json` | Optional. `gpu: "metal"` or `"full"` for WebGL styles, `schedule: "contiguous"` for path-dependent simulations, `eventsGpu` if `events.mjs` needs a GPU mode, `signal` to have `events.mjs` save a raw sample stream from `window.signal()` instead of `events.json`. |
| `audio.py` | Synthesises the soundtrack from `events.json` (the cue list `window.events()` returns). Sounds are generated, never sampled. A silent stereo track is acceptable. |
| `build.sh` | Rebuilds the clip end to end: frames into a temporary folder → `events.json` → `audio.wav` → `<slug>.mp4` → `preview.gif`, then deletes the frames and the wav. |
| `meta.json` | Number, name, `feel` (six words or fewer), `best_for` (ten words or fewer), `family`, `use_cases` and optional `credits` (`role`, `name`, `url`). |
| `fonts/`, `fonts.css` | Self-hosted webfonts. Only fonts whose license allows redistribution; list them in [FONTS.md](FONTS.md). |
| `vendor/`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` | Any third-party code (for example three.js or rough.js), vendored locally and credited with its license. Nothing loads from the network at render time. |
| `skills/animating-in-video-styles/references/styles/<slug>.md` | The style's recipe for the agent skill, written from the code against [RECIPE_TEMPLATE.md](RECIPE_TEMPLATE.md): signature, palette, type, texture, motion, sound, a reuse map of the code, adaptation notes and boundaries. It lives in the skill, outside the style folder, so the skill installs on its own. |

`README.md` in the style folder, the gallery in the root README, the pages under `categories/` and the
skill's `references/catalog.md` and `assets/catalog.json` are generated by `build_index.py`; do not edit them
by hand.

## Encoding

- Video: H.264, 30 fps, CRF 18, full range (`yuvj420p`, colour range `pc`), as JPEG frames
  give it. Keep the file under about 40 MB: static or slowly refreshed grain compresses far
  better than noise that changes every frame.
- Audio: AAC, 48 kHz, stereo.
- Preview: `preview.gif` at 480 px wide and 6 fps with a 96-colour palette and Bayer
  dithering, the same filter `make_previews.py` uses.

## Pipeline

1. Each style is built in its own git worktree and branch (`style/<slug>`) and merged with
   its own pull request. Style commits only touch `styles/<slug>/` and the style's recipe.
2. Precompute expensive textures, blur and grain once into offscreen canvases. Render with at
   most two browser pages in parallel.
3. Review a contact sheet (about 16–20 frames tiled) and full-resolution key frames, critique
   them against this brief, and make at least two correction passes.
4. Write the recipe (or update it when a clip is remade) and have it reviewed by someone other than its
   author.
5. Run `./build.sh`, then `python3 build_index.py && python3 check_catalog.py`. Every line
   must end in `ok`: the clip's codec, size, frame rate, range, duration and audio, the
   preview, the index entries and the shared scripts are all checked.
6. The catalog reel (`catalog/catalog.mp4`) is rebuilt with `combine.py` after styles are
   added or remade. It is git-ignored: upload it as the `catalog.mp4` asset of a new release
   (`gh release create vX.Y.Z catalog/catalog.mp4`).
