## Memory Fading Into Watercolor (techniques adapted)

The pigment-transport simulation (noise-weighted max-dilation of wet fronts over paper "thirst")
and the `pigment()` edge-pooling / back-run / granulation model with Beer–Lambert compositing in
`anim.html` are adapted from "Memory Fading Into Watercolor" by Techartist,
https://github.com/iamtechartist/memory-fading-into-watercolor. The scene, drawing, timeline,
colour design and audio here are original.
