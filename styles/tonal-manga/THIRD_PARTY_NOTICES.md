# Third-party notices — tonal-manga

## Shippori Mincho SemiBold (font, `fonts/ShipporiMincho-normal-600-latin.woff2`)

By FONTDASU, from Google Fonts (Latin subset), SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used for the title, the only text in the clip.

Everything else is procedural and original: the street corner, the utility poles and wires, the crow, the balcony and its laundry, the evening rooftops and the moon, every dot screen, line tone and brush contour, and the synthesised audio. The look is inspired by the screentone tradition of Japanese manga in general; no existing page, panel or artwork is reproduced.
