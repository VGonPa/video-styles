# Third-party notices — cubism

## Stardos Stencil (font, `fonts/StardosStencil-700-latin.woff2`)

By Vernon Adams, from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used for the stencilled title and subtitle, rasterised as a mask and brush-stippled on.

The guitar model, the facets and their viewpoints, the passage shading and hatching, the collage papers (faux-bois, grey-blue paper and newsprint), the canvas and paper textures and the synthesized audio are all procedurally generated and original. The look is inspired by the Cubist painters in general; no existing painting is reproduced.
