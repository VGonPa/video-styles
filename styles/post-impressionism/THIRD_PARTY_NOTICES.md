## Fraunces (fonts, `fonts/Fraunces-900-latin.woff2`, `fonts/Fraunces-italic-latin.woff2`)

By The Fraunces Project Authors (Undercase Type). The Black cut carries the painted title; the variable italic sets the dates under it.

Everything else (the linen weave, the brush-mark atlas, the Prussian-blue outlines, the wheat field, haystacks and path, the turning day and night skies, the sun, the stars, the painted title fill and the synthesized audio) is original and procedural.
