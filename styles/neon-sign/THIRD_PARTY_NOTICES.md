# Third-party notices — neon-sign

## Hershey vector fonts (glyph centerlines in `glyphs.js`)

The single-stroke tube paths for "Good Ideas" (Hershey *scripts*), "SERVED HERE", "OPEN" and "LATE" (Hershey *futural*)
were exported with `tools/export_glyphs.py` from the Hershey fonts as bundled in the `Hershey-Fonts` Python package (MIT).

The Hershey fonts were created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U.S. National Bureau of Standards.
They are in the public domain; the customary credit is reproduced here:

> The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards.
> The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by James Hurt, Cognition, Inc.

No other third-party code, fonts, images or sounds are used; textures and audio are generated procedurally.
