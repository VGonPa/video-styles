By Pablo Impallari and Rodrigo Fuenzalida, from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used for every display word, the title, the chart labels and the asterisk incised on the plaster solid; the words are also rasterised into signed distance fields for the morphs.

## Libre Caslon Text italic (font, `fonts/LibreCaslonText-italic-400-latin.woff2`)

By Pablo Impallari and Rodrigo Fuenzalida, from Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used for the small plate label.

The celestial chart, the skull, the constructed capital C, the nautilus section, the Doric capital, the engraved and the plaster rhombicuboctahedron, all their hatching and stippling, and the synthesized audio are procedurally generated and original. The look is inspired by 16th–18th-century engraved plates in general; no existing plate or artwork is reproduced.
